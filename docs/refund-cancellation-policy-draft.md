# Refund & Subscription Cancellation Policy

**PixenarAI · Pixenar Studio · PixenarMV**  
Operated by Pensrikris36 LLC  
**Effective date:** Pending publication and legal review.

## Subscription cancellation
You may cancel your subscription at any time through the available account controls or customer support. Cancellation prevents future renewal charges; it does not automatically refund the current billing period. Access and remaining credits follow the purchase terms disclosed at checkout and applicable law.

## AI generation credits
AI generation uses computational resources and third-party processing. Credits spent on services successfully generated **and delivered as advertised** are generally not refundable solely because the customer changes their mind or dislikes the subjective creative result. Credit consumption alone does not establish successful delivery.

## Refund review
We review refund requests individually. A full or partial refund, restored credits, or another appropriate remedy may be provided for verified duplicate or erroneous billing, failed or inaccessible output, a materially unavailable or misrepresented advertised feature, or where applicable law requires a remedy. Where some credits remain unused, we may consider a partial refund based on the purchase terms and services delivered.

## Failed generations
For verified technical or provider failures, we will investigate and may restore affected credits, retry generation, or issue an appropriate refund. Missing, corrupted, inaccessible, or materially nonconforming outputs are not automatically treated as successfully delivered.

## Requesting assistance
Contact the support channel shown in your account or checkout receipt with the account email, transaction details, and issue description. We aim to acknowledge requests within two business days and provide an initial decision within five business days, without limiting legal or payment-network deadlines.

## Approved refunds
Where supported, approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. Unused credits attributable to a refunded purchase may be revoked to avoid duplicate entitlements. We will not treat a new policy as retroactively accepted for earlier purchases.

## Disputes and legal rights
You may exercise lawful payment disputes and consumer protection rights. Nothing in this policy excludes rights that cannot be waived by law. We may update the policy prospectively; the terms properly disclosed at the time of purchase govern, subject to applicable law.

**Implementation note:** This draft requires legal review, a clearly displayed effective date, checkout disclosure, non-prechecked acceptance, and recorded policy-version acceptance before it is relied upon as an enforceable purchase term.
