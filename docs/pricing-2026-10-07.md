# Pricing and billing policy — 2026-10-07
Target contribution margin is 62.5% against the lowest net credit value, rounded upward to whole credits. This is a budget model, not an absolute no-loss guarantee.

Net credit value = (79.99 × 0.92 − 0.30) / 3500 = $0.02094022857142857.
The 8% plus $0.30 payment allowance is conservative and must be checked against actual Stripe fees, billing/Tax charges and the supported payment methods.
Cost budget = verified Runway list cost × 1.20 + $0.02 per request. The 20% plus $0.02 reserve covers provider tax and infrastructure only up to that budget. Storage beyond retention, chargebacks, FX, unusual tax rates and promotions are not guaranteed by this allowance.
Credits = ceil(cost budget / (net credit value × 0.375)).

WAN now uses Runway: Standard $0.05/$0.10/$0.20 per second; Prime $0.068/$0.14/$0.28, for 480p/720p/1080p. Both are quoted for each supported duration from 2 through 30 seconds. Historical Alibaba jobs retain their original provider.
Pricing includes minimum billable costs, image reference surcharges and Veo audio selection. No arbitrary client-selected resolution, reference count, professional format or video input is allowed to bypass the priced request shape.
Payment packs are unchanged. Credit balances are not rescaled again. Client pricing version must match before debit.
Provider-billed SAFETY.INPUT failures retain charges. Confirmed refundable Runway failures refund once; missing billing evidence and uncertain submit/timeouts retain reservations for manual reconciliation. No automatic paid submit retries.
Unverified video editing/upscale and realtime-avatar billing flows remain paused before debit.
The API permits administrator testing, which is an owner-funded expense. Referral/bonus/free credits, subscription discounts and refunds/chargebacks need a separately funded budget; they cannot be claimed to earn 60–65% on every individual job.
Sources: https://docs.dev.runwayml.com/guides/pricing/ and https://docs.dev.runwayml.com/errors/task-failures/ and https://stripe.com/pricing
